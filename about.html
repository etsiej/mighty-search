About, privacy and disclaimer

What this is

Mighty Search is a free, non-commercial tool for finding hardware, tools and building supplies, browsing by department, and seeing which stores list a product in stock. It also keeps a daily record of prices for discounted products so you can see how a price has moved.

Unofficial

Product, price and store information comes from the search service published on Mitre 10 NZ's own website. Mitre 10 and all related names, logos and images belong to their respective owners, and product photos are loaded from Mitre 10's servers and belong to Mitre 10 or its suppliers.

Pricing is indicative only

Prices, discounts, price history, "deal" labels and stock information on this site are indicative only. They may be wrong, incomplete or out of date, and they are national prices, whereas individual stores can price differently. Do not rely on them to make a purchase or any other decision, and do not copy, scrape or reuse the pricing data. Always check the current price and availability with the store or on the retailer's own website before you buy.

Stock shown is a snapshot taken when the retailer's search data was last updated, not live inventory. The "is this a good deal?" notes are automated comparisons against the days we have tracked and are not advice.

The information is provided as is, without any warranty. To the extent the law allows, the operator accepts no liability for loss arising from its use.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. It does not collect or store what you search for.

  • Your searches go to Algolia. Search, browsing and the deals list run directly from your browser to Algolia, the search provider used by the retailer's own website. Algolia receives what you type and the filters you choose, together with your IP address and standard browser details, as it would for any visitor to the retailer's own site. This site never sees or keeps that data. See Algolia's privacy policy.
  • Product photos come from the retailer. Your browser loads images from the retailer's servers, which can see your IP address.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log requests such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Your store is saved on your device only. The store you pick is kept in your browser's local storage so you don't have to choose it again. It is never sent to this site. Clear it with "Clear my store", or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Links you share contain your search, filters and chosen store in the web address (after the #), so anyone you send a link to can see them.
