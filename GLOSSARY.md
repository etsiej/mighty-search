# Mighty Search

An unofficial, publicly hosted product search for a New Zealand hardware retailer, for people whose browser protections break the official site's search.

## Language

**Store**:
A physical branch of the retailer, identified by its store number (e.g. 31 = Thames).
_Avoid_: Branch, shop, location

**Nationwide**:
A product that the retailer can supply anywhere in New Zealand, as flagged by the product data.
_Avoid_: Online-only, national

**My store**:
The one Store a visitor has chosen to shop at, remembered in their browser.
_Avoid_: Preferred store, selected store, local store

**In stock**:
A product that the product data lists as having stock at the visitor's My store. Reflects the last data update, not live inventory.
_Avoid_: Available, in store, on shelf

## Prices

**List price**:
A product's normal national price (the retailer's "RRP"), before any promotion.
_Avoid_: RRP, original price, was price

**Deal**:
A product whose promo price is strictly below its list price. "Everyday Low Price" items, where the two are equal, are not deals.
_Avoid_: Special, sale item, offer

**Tracked product**:
A product whose price changes we record daily, starting the first day we see it on a Deal.
_Avoid_: Watched product, monitored product
